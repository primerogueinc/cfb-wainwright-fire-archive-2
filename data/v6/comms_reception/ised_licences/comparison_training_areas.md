## What it shows
- **DND licenses almost nothing, everywhere.** Military radio doesn't show up at any of the six. The only DND licences are "Excon" (exercise control) microwave links at Wainwright and Suffield, the two Alberta army manoeuvre and live-fire centres.
- **Wainwright isn't unusual for radio.** Its public-safety and cell picture is mid-range: better covered than Suffield, thinner than the garrison towns of Petawawa, Valcartier and Shilo.
- **Gagetown, at the fire site, has fewer licensed public-safety stations within 30 km than Wainwright.** New Brunswick's provincial radio system (NBTMRS) doesn't appear by name, just as AFRRCS doesn't in Alberta. Provincial trunked systems are missing from the public extract in both provinces.
- **Suffield stands out** for many civilian contractor licences on the range itself (drone and target-system testing) and almost no cell service near the centre.
- **Weatheradio** is 12–52 km away at all six. Wainwright (49 km) and Petawawa (52 km) are the farthest.
